Who We Are

A Movement Rooted in Compassion & Collective Action

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, operating across multiple Indian states.

In a world that often feels divided, InAmigos stands as a beacon for unity, addressing critical societal gaps through education, food distribution, women's empowerment, animal welfare, and environmental sustainability. With a network of dedicated volunteers and transparent operations, every contribution fuels real, measurable change.

✔ Section 8 Registered ✔ 80G & 12A Certified ✔ CSR-1 Registered ✔ NITI Aayog Registered ✔ ISO 9001:2015 Certified
Our Projects

Six Initiatives. One Mission.

Each project targets a distinct societal challenge, working together to build a more inclusive and sustainable future.

Project SEVA

Distributing meals, clothing, and essential supplies to the homeless and underprivileged. More than 50,000+ meals have been served through regular food drives and donation campaigns.

Social Impact

Why Our Work Matters

India faces deep-rooted challenges — food insecurity, educational inequality, gender disparity, environmental degradation. InAmigos confronts each one with structured, accountable, and community-led programs.

Breaking the cycle of poverty through education

Bachpanshala ensures no child is left behind due to limited resources or access to digital tools.

Strengthening women-led communities

Udaan gives rural women the skills and confidence to achieve financial independence and advocate for their rights.

Protecting the planet for future generations

Prakriti's grassroots plantation drives and eco-agriculture support directly combat deforestation and climate impact.

Building employment pathways for youth

Vikas channels young energy into structured internships, turning aspiration into real-world capability.
